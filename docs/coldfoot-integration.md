# Coldfoot run-1 1×1 integration

The carrier uses wafer.space's **run-1 `1x1-cob/1x1-mezzanine`**, per the user's
selection. The earlier run-2 half-slot mismatch is superseded by this selection.
Vendor CAD is retained under `hardware/vendor/wafer-space/`.

[Run-1 audit](run1-module-pin-audit.csv) records actual PCB nets.
[Coldfoot mapping](coldfoot-run1-map.csv) joins them to the local
`asic/librelane/slots/slot_1x1.yaml`: south, east, reversed north, reversed west.
All 74 die-pad positions and fixed supply/ground positions were checked. The
70-pin mating connector is HCTL `HC-PBB40C-70DS-0.4V-3.0-02`. Its footprint comes
from the upstream motherboard, with pad rotations normalized from its placement.
The receptacle has 70 electrical contacts; plug mounting pads are not extra HAT pins.

The module is 14 × 16 mm with a selected 3 mm mating stack. Verify mating samples,
module thickness, encapsulation clearance and retention before fabrication. A
module assembled for Coldfoot must use the CSV bond map; the generic PCB alone
does not establish which chip is fitted or how it is bonded.

## Supporting circuit

- AP63203 fixed 3.3 V buck: two 10 uF input caps, 100 nF bootstrap, 4.7 uH
  SRN6045TA-4R7M and three 22 uF output caps. The typical circuit uses 3.9 uH;
  4.7 uH is within the stated 2.2–10 uH range and was checked in a bounded
  switching power-stage simulation.
- A 0.1 ohm current shunt feeds `CF_3V3`, with 22 uF bulk and eight 100 nF local
  bypass caps, in addition to capacitors on the run-1 module.
- Core, four `VDD_IO` connections and both `PWR_AUX` connections receive 3.3 V.
  **The AUX positions map to additional DVDD pads in Coldfoot.** This follows
  the selected 3.3 V standard-cell, SRAM and I/O configuration.
- Two independent ASE-25.000MHZ-L-C-T oscillators provide 25 MHz mesh/tile clocks,
  each with 100 nF bypass and 33 ohm source resistance. This matches the selected
  `chip_top.sdc`; neither clock is generated by Linux GPIO toggling.
- TLV803EA30DBZR provides a 3.0 V threshold, nominal 200 ms reset release and
  open-drain output. A 10 kohm pull-up defines reset high. GPIO17 **high asserts
  reset** through a 2N7002, with 1 kohm gate resistor and 100 kohm pulldown.
- Unused digital inputs and bidirectional pads have explicit 100 kohm biasing;
  QSPI CS pads bias high, other unused digital pads low. Reserved analog pads
  remain open. External multi-chip QSPI peers are not connected.

The regulator's 2 A rating is not the module's allowed load. Operating envelope:
600 mA total, at most 500 mA combined core contact current, at most 300 mA per
connector contact. Measure actual chip power against these limits. Budget roughly
0.7 A additional Pi 5 V input current at the stated upper loads, excluding the Pi.

## Host connection

TXU0202DCUR isolates the Pi and Coldfoot UART power domains. GPIO25 enables it;
a 100 kohm pulldown disables it at boot. GPIO14 sends, GPIO15 receives. The chip
RX path has 47 ohm series damping and a 10 kohm pull-up to hold idle when disabled.

| Module pin | Role | HAT net |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | mesh clock | CF_MESH_CLK |
| 7 | reset | CF_RESET_N |
| 27 | input[0], host UART RX | CF_UART_RX |
| 6 | bidir[0], host UART TX | CF_UART_TX |
| 22 | input[3], tile clock | CF_TILE_CLK |

The CSV is authoritative for the full map. J4 is a ground/UART RX/UART TX/reset
test header attached to the module, with no power input. Do not attach a second
driving UART transmitter.

Use the existing runtime contract and BundleImage v8. Coldfoot is not a generic
raw-tensor accelerator. Its selected host UART is 2 Mbaud with nine-byte frames
(`A5 5A 04 00`, four data bytes, XOR8). The Pi must acquire, preprocess and encode
sensor data. No runtime or RTL changes were made for this PCB.
